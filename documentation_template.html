1.0 Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Severe convective weather events—such as sudden thunderstorms, damaging hailstorms, cloudburst flash floods, and violent microbursts/downbursts—are characterized by rapid initiation (<15–30 minutes) and extreme spatial localization (1–10 km). Traditional Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) models (e.g., GFS, NCUM) operate on 3- to 6-hour assimilation cycles with 4–12 km grids, making them incapable of resolving localized convective genesis in real time.

Mission of TropoTrace: To deliver an operator-grade, automated Convective Intelligence Command Center operating in the 0 to 6 hour nowcast horizon at 1–3 km spatial resolution, integrating Doppler Weather Radar, INSAT-3DR Geostationary satellite feeds, lightning discharge rates, and AWS surface stations.

Latitude = 13.6 - (y × 0.045)  |  Longitude = 74.8 + (x × 0.045)
Geographic Extent: Latitude 10.0°N to 13.6°N  •  Longitude 74.8°E to 79.3°E

4.2 Location Permissions & Geolocation Handling

When an operator activates the "MY LOCATION" tool on the radar toolbar:

  • The application calls the W3C standard navigator.geolocation.getCurrentPosition() with enableHighAccuracy: true.
  • The browser prompts the user for sensor permissions.
  • Permission Granted: State transitions from LOCATING to FOUND. The map executes a smooth flyTo() animation to the user's coordinates, renders a 650-meter cyan accuracy radius, and shifts the radar range rings relative to their vantage point.
  • Permission Denied: Transitions to DENIED without crashing, maintaining default radar focus.

5.0 The Convective Intelligence & ML Nowcasting Engine

Implemented in simulation.ts, TropoTrace models physical convective storm genesis, cell kinematics, and multi-sensor consensus based on proven meteorological algorithms.

5.1 Cell Identification & Kinematic Tracking (TITAN / SCIT)

Storm cells are detected by clustering contiguous Doppler radar reflectivity cores above 40 dBZ. The centroid coordinates are calculated over time to derive translation speed ($v$) and azimuth direction ($\theta$). Cells are categorized into 5 lifecycle stages:

Lifecycle State Machine:
• INITIATING (Intensity < 38%) → Developing updraft, first radar echo.
• DEVELOPING (38% – 55%) → Rapid cloud top cooling, lightning onset.
• INTENSIFYING (56% – 73%) → Lightning jump, reflectivity core aloft.
• MATURE (74% – 88%) → Peak dBZ, heavy precipitation, hail/downburst outflow.
• DECAYING (> 88%) → Downdraft dominance, core collapse.

5.2 Multi-Hazard Mathematical Formulations

  • Composite Risk ($R_{\text{comp}}$): $\text{clamp}(0.90 \cdot \text{Intensity} + 0.16 \cdot \text{LightningRate})$
  • Equivalent Reflectivity ($Z$): $\text{clamp}(40 + 0.52 \cdot \text{Intensity})\ \text{dBZ}$ ($>55\ \text{dBZ}$ signals severe hail core).
  • Hail Probability ($P_{\text{hail}}$): Triggered when reflectivity aloft exceeds 55 dBZ above the freezing level (+4.2 km) with high Vertically Integrated Liquid (VIL $>45\ \text{kg/m}^2$).
  • Downburst Wind ($V_{\text{gust}}$): Estimated outflow velocity: $32 + 0.42 \cdot \text{Intensity}\ \text{km/h}$.
  • Cloudburst Trigger: Rainfall rate $>100\ \text{mm/h}$ concentrated over a localized drainage basin.

5.3 Multi-Sensor Consensus & Degradation Protocol

The platform samples 5 telemetry feeds. If Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) experiences latency (>12 minutes), the system flags it as DELAYED, drops the consensus score, reduces confidence by 11%, surfaces an advisory (*"Forecast confidence reduced"*), and dynamically re-weights real-time radar and lightning observations.

8.0 Judge Defense Q&A — Technical Presentation Guide

Q1 How does TropoTrace differ from commercial apps like AccuWeather or Windy?
Standard consumer weather apps present synoptic forecasts hours in advance on coarse 4–12 km grids. TropoTrace is a Convective-Scale Nowcasting Decision-Support System operating in the 0 to 6 hour horizon at 1–3 km resolution. It tracks discrete storm centroids, detects lightning jumps before surface impact, projects trajectory uncertainty cones, models infrastructure exposure (hospitals, schools, substations), and produces CAP v1.2 alerts in 8 Indian languages.
Q2 How do you guarantee that the Groq AI Copilot does not hallucinate?
We enforce a strict Zero-Hallucination Grounding Protocol. The active scenario snapshot (dBZ reflectivity, lightning rate, rainfall accumulation, sensor latencies, and exposed demographic tallies) is injected into the model's system prompt. The model is explicitly constrained from guessing. Any question about unmonitored parameters triggers: "Insufficient current data in current scenario snapshot." An automated regex parser validates the text and binds physical instrument citation pills (Doppler S-Band, GLDN, INSAT-3DR) to every claim.
Q3 What algorithms are used for storm cell tracking and trajectory projection?
Our engine is modeled on the TITAN (Thunderstorm Identification, Tracking, Analysis, and Nowcasting) and SCIT (Storm Cell Identification and Tracking) algorithms developed by NCAR and NOAA. Reflectivity clusters $>40\ \text{dBZ}$ are grouped into centroids, and temporal vectors compute speed and azimuth direction. Instead of a single straight line, we project a widening uncertainty cone polygon downstream to account for atmospheric steering shear and lateral storm propagation.
Q4 Why is a lightning jump an important forecasting signal?
A lightning jump (a sudden spike in flash discharge $>45\ \text{fl/min}$) is a direct physical indicator of an intensifying convective updraft. Updraft acceleration lofts supercooled water droplets above the freezing level, causing rapid non-inductive graupel-ice charge separation. Research by Schultz et al. demonstrates that lightning jumps precede severe surface hazards (damaging hail, microbursts, and cloudburst downpours) by 15 to 25 minutes, providing an early warning lead time before radar cores peak.
Q5 How does the system handle telemetry failures or sensor latency?
TropoTrace includes a Multi-Sensor Consensus & Degradation Protocol. It monitors 5 feeds (Radar, Satellite, Lightning, AWS, and NWP). If a stream lags (e.g., NWP model guidance delayed $>12$ minutes), the system marks it as DELAYED, drops the consensus score, reduces confidence from 93% to 78%, surfaces an advisory (*"Forecast confidence reduced"*), and dynamically re-weights remaining real-time radar and lightning streams.
Q6 What is the cloud database architecture?
TropoTrace utilizes Firebase Auth for operator identity and Cloud Firestore as a serverless NoSQL document database. Data is partitioned under users/{userId} with sub-collections for savedAlerts and scenarioRuns. Firestore security rules enforce strict user-level tenancy, preventing unauthorized cross-tenant access, while Firebase Hosting delivers the static SPA across global edge CDNs.
